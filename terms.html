Inclusions

For this package, Pilotos Grounds agrees to the following:

  1. Render service of at least 3 hours and a maximum of 4 hours, including ingress and egress.
  2. Complete set of equipment to fulfill its obligations.
  3. Provide 3 to 4 baristas to serve the drinks.
  4. Serve a max of 100 drinks, limited only to the agreed menu inclusions with the client.

Should the team be required to render additional hours, a fee of ₱1,000.00 will be applied, and should any additional cups be served, each cup will be sold at the rate per cup of the package chosen.

E.g. The package for 50 cups is priced at ₱180.00 per cup, therefore any additional cups served will incur a fee of ₱180.00 each.

Location Requirements

If applicable, a copy of a Special Event Permit must be obtained by Pilotos Grounds no later than a week (7 days) prior to the event. Clients are responsible for ensuring the event location can meet the following requirements:

  • Complimentary and accessible parking for at least 1 vehicle. Vehicle plate numbers will be declared at least 48 hours prior to the event; if suitable parking isn't available and the team must park illegally, the client is responsible for any resulting fines.
  • Lift access (ramps, elevators, etc.) for the cart, since equipment is transported through the mobile cart itself.
  • Electricity supply of at least 1,500W near the cart location for our espresso machine. We provide a 6-meter extension cord; if the power supply is further, the client must disclose this at least 24 hours prior so we can bring additional equipment. A generator or extra extension cords, if required, will incur additional charges.
  • Shade in case of unfavorable weather. Pilotos Grounds reserves the right, in good faith, to pause operation if weather poses a danger to personnel, equipment, or guests · compensation is not affected if the operation ends for this reason.

Pilotos Grounds has the right to cancel a booking should the location fail to meet these requirements, and the cancellation/refund policy above will apply.

If meals cannot be provided in-kind, the client agrees to provide a meal allowance of ₱250.00 per staff member, settled with the final payment or during the event. Meals should be of reasonable quality and served at an appropriate time to allow short breaks without disrupting operations.

Failure to provide meals or the agreed allowance may result in additional charges and/or limited staff rotation, which may affect service efficiency.
