Inclusions

In consideration of this package, Pilotos Grounds shall provide the following:

  1. Service of not less than 3 hours and not more than 4 hours, inclusive of ingress and egress.
  2. A complete set of equipment necessary to fulfill its obligations under this agreement.
  3. A staff of 3 to 4 baristas to prepare and serve the beverages.
  4. A maximum of 100 beverages, limited solely to the menu inclusions agreed upon with the Client.

Should the team be required to render service beyond the agreed duration, a fee of ₱1,000.00 shall apply. Should additional cups be served beyond the agreed quantity, each such cup shall be charged at the per-cup rate of the package selected.

For example, the package for 50 cups is priced at ₱180.00 per cup; accordingly, any cups served in excess of that quantity shall be charged at ₱180.00 each.

Location Requirements

Where applicable, a copy of a Special Event Permit must be obtained by Pilotos Grounds no later than seven (7) days prior to the event. The Client shall be responsible for ensuring that the event location satisfies the following requirements:

  • Complimentary and accessible parking for at least one (1) vehicle. Vehicle plate numbers shall be declared at least 48 hours prior to the event; should suitable parking not be available and the team be compelled to park in an unauthorized manner, the Client shall be responsible for any resulting fines.
  • Lift access (ramps, elevators, or similar means) for the cart, as equipment is transported by way of the mobile cart itself.
  • An electricity supply of at least 1,500W in proximity to the cart location for the espresso machine. Pilotos Grounds shall provide a 6-meter extension cord; should the power supply be located farther than this distance, the Client must disclose this at least 24 hours in advance so that additional equipment may be arranged. A generator or additional extension cords, where required, shall incur additional charges.
