Brief History

From two baristas
to a whole movement.

01

The Name

"Pilotos" is derived from the name "Pilot" · representing our founder. The propeller in our mark is a nod to that story, reimagined as a coffee bean.

02

Nov 12, 2022

We proudly opened our first official shop in Batangas City, marking the start of Pilotos Grounds as we now know it.

03

2 → 30

In just two years, our team grew from 2 baristas to 30 members · powering both our home shop and our mobile event cart.

04

Our Mission

To deliver delicious, affordable coffee that keeps you coming back. We can't promise perfection · every cup is made with hard work and a commitment to excellence.

05

Today

A shop, a mobile cart for your events, a bottled Grounds On The Go line, merch worn by #TEAMPILOTOS, and Afterhours · our late-night sister venue.
